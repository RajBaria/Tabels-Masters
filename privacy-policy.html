Privacy Policy

App: Tables Master | Package: com.rajkumarbaria.tablesmaster
Developer: Rajkumar Baria | Effective Date: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tables Master ("the App"). We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring a safe, transparent educational experience for learners of all ages, including children and students.

2. Information Collection and Storage

We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or rent any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • Local On-Device Storage: All learning progress, profiles, scores, and mistake history are stored strictly locally on your device via an internal SQLite/Room database.
  • Security PIN: Student profile security PINs are protected with one-way salted SHA-256 cryptographic hashing locally on your device.
  • Google Drive Backup: Cloud backup is completely optional and user-initiated. If used, data is exported directly to your private Google Drive account. We do not operate external intermediate servers.

3. Permissions and Usage

Permission Usage & Purpose
android.permission.VIBRATE Provides haptic feedback during quiz interactions. Can be toggled off in settings anytime.
android.permission.INTERNET Used strictly for optional Google Drive backup sync and opening developer social links.

4. Text-to-Speech (Audio Voice Recitation)

Tables Master utilizes your device's native Android Text-to-Speech service (such as Google Speech Services) to recite multiplication tables, squares, cubes, and quiz questions aloud. No audio or voice recordings are ever created or transmitted.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Tables Master is a family-friendly educational application suitable for all ages:

  • No personal information is collected from children under 13.
  • No third-party advertisements or behavioral targeted advertising.
  • No behavioral tracking or data sharing with third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored locally, you maintain complete ownership. You can clear mistake history, delete individual profiles, or uninstall the application at any time to permanently remove all stored data.
