## 3. Full Description (Google Play Store Ready)

Unlock the power of mental math with **Tables Master** — the ultimate interactive, audio-powered learning companion for students, parents, and math enthusiasts!

Master multiplication tables (1 to 30), squares ($n^2$), cubes ($n^3$), square roots ($\sqrt{n}$), and cube roots ($\sqrt[3]{n}$) with authentic voice recitation in **English, Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), Hindi (हिन्दी), Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali**.

---

### 🌟 Key Features

#### 1. 📊 Complete Math Modules (1 to 30)
- **Multiplication Tables (ઘડિયા):** Full tables from 1 to 30 with line-by-line recitation and full continuous reading mode.
- **Squares (વર્ગ - $n^2$):** Complete squares from $1^2 = 1$ to $30^2 = 900$.
- **Cubes (ઘન - $n^3$):** Complete cubes from $1^3 = 1$ to $30^3 = 27,000$.
- **Square Roots (વર્ગમૂળ - $\sqrt{n}$):** Instant square roots from $\sqrt{1} = 1$ to $\sqrt{900} = 30$.
- **Cube Roots (ઘનમૂળ - $\sqrt[3]{n}$):** Instant cube roots from $\sqrt[3]{1} = 1$ to $\sqrt[3]{27,000} = 30$.

#### 2. ⚡ Audio Test Arena & Running Quizzes
- **Rapid-Fire Audio Quizzes:** Listen to spoken math questions and answer with lightning speed!
- **Palakha (પલાખા) Range Selection:** Practice questions from **1 to 10**, **11 to 20**, **21 to 30**, or **Random 1 to 30**.
- **Interactive Sliders:** Smooth slider controls to quickly select any table or customize speech speed (0.5x to 2.5x).
- **Multiple Difficulty Levels:** Easy (8s), Medium (5s), Hard (3s), and Lightning Fast (2s).
- **Instant Audio & Haptic Feedback:** Get spoken confirmation and gentle vibration on answers.

#### 3. 🎙️ 8 Unique Voice Styles & 7 Languages
Choose from 8 distinct voice profiles:
- 👩 Gentle Female
- 👨 Deep Male
- 👧 Young Girl
- 👦 Young Student
- 👩‍🏫 Classroom Teacher
- 👨‍🏫 Senior Professor
- ⚡ Speed Trainer
- 🎯 Calm Precision

Available in **English, Gujarati, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali**!

#### 4. 📈 Real-Time Analytics Dashboard
- Track total tests completed, overall accuracy %, and average response speed.
- **Table Mastery Matrix (1 to 30):** Visual heat map showing strengths and learning gaps.
- **Mistake Practice Drill:** One-tap targeted drill to practice only your past mistakes.

#### 5. 👥 Multi-Student Profiles & Local Security PIN
- Create multiple student profiles on a single family device.
- Optional 4-digit PIN protection to keep each learner's progress private.

#### 6. ☁️ Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)
- Backup your learning reports and profile data directly into your personal Google Drive account.
- 100% offline-first: works without internet, with optional user-initiated cloud restore.
