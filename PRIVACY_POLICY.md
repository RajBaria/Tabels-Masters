## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Tables Master** ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, secure educational experience for users of all ages, including children and students. 

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Tables Master application.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Personal Information
- **We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or rent any personally identifiable information (PII).**
- You do not need to register an account with us to use the App.

### B. Local Device Data (Offline-First)
All learning progress and educational data are stored **locally on your device** using an internal Android SQLite/Room database:
- Student profile names and avatar selections
- Local security PINs (hashed using one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hashing)
- Quiz scores, response speed, accuracy percentages, and mistake logs
- Language and voice preferences

This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

### C. Optional Google Account & Google Drive Backup
- If you voluntarily choose to use the Google Drive Backup feature, the app allows you to export your learning reports directly to your personal Google Drive storage.
- The App only interacts with your private Google Drive storage for backup and restoration purposes. We have no access to your Google account credentials or your private Drive files.

---

## 3. Device Permissions and Usage

Tables Master requests only minimal, necessary Android system permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`android.permission.VIBRATE`** | Provides gentle haptic feedback on button presses and quiz answer submissions. Can be toggled on/off in Settings. |
| **`android.permission.INTERNET`** | Used exclusively for the optional user-initiated Google Drive cloud backup and opening external developer social links if tapped. |

The app does **not** request access to your camera, microphone, device location, contacts, or broad file storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`).

---

## 4. Text-to-Speech (Audio Voice Recitation)
The App uses the standard Android operating system `TextToSpeech` service (such as Google Speech Services / Google TTS) installed on your device to recite math tables, squares, cubes, and quiz questions aloud. No audio or voice recordings are captured, uploaded, or transmitted.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
Tables Master is an educational application designed for learners of all ages, including children under 13:
- We do **not** collect personal information from children.
- We do **not** display third-party advertisements or behavioral targeted ads.
- We do **not** engage in cross-app tracking or user profiling.
- Local student PIN protection is provided so parents and educators can secure student profiles on shared family devices.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services
Tables Master does not integrate third-party analytics trackers, ad networks, or marketing SDKs.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- You can delete any student profile, reset your quiz mistake history, or clear your data at any time from within the App.
- Uninstalling the App or clearing app data in Android Settings will permanently remove all locally stored progress.
